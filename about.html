About Us

The team behind SkillSwap, and the mentor who guided us there.

Our Story

SkillSwap was built by Team Spidey Sensi — Harish, Khusraj, Ashaz, Vighneswar, and Bhavin — for the Design Championship 2026. What started as a shared idea grew into a full platform through late nights, countless revisions, and a lot of learning from each other along the way, which felt fitting for a project built entirely around the idea of trading skills. Every line of code, every design decision, and every feature carries a piece of what this team taught one another. None of it would have come together without the guidance of our mentor, Spoorthi Paladugu, who supported us at every step, pushed us to think harder, and helped turn a rough idea into something we're proud to share.
