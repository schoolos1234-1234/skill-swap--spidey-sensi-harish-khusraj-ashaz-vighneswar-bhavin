How SkillSwap Works

From your profile to your first swap in four simple steps.

1

Create Your Profile

Tell the community what makes you, you. Add a short bio, your location, and a note about what you're excited to learn.

2

Post What You Can Teach

List a skill, choose your level, and say what you'd like in return. Most people finish a listing in about two minutes.

3

Browse & Connect

Search by category, level, or keyword, then send a swap request to someone whose skill catches your eye.

4

Meet, Learn, and Grow

Agree on the details, hop on a call or meet up, swap your skills, then rate the experience for the community.

🎯

Be Specific

"Beginner Guitar Chords in 4 Weeks" gets more replies than just "Guitar." Details build trust.

🔄

Be Flexible

The best swaps aren't always a perfect one-for-one trade. Stay open to creative exchanges.

💬

Be Kind

A friendly message and a little patience go a long way toward turning a request into a great swap.
